List of functions:
1. run_replicates (simulates n runs of the experiment) - Ashna
2. run_simulation (one run that builds the starting colony) - Ashna
3. get_dose (applies a given antibiotic dosage and survival outcomes are determined by rules) - Ashna
4. growth_step (simulates one growth step and handles division after dosage) - Ashna
5. count_update (updates cell counts after each dose and growth step) - Ashna
6. mutation_uptake (allocates mutations to cells individually) - Ashna
7. dose_sweep (required to generate one output; takes a list of doses, and returns one set of results) - Rachana
8. graph_1 (first output, this maps the population of alive and resistant cells as a function of time) - Rachana
9. graph_2 (second output, maps the results of dose_sweep, which can be probability of developing resistance or resistant fraction as a function of dosage) - Rachana
10. render_video (third output, generates the animation for one run). - Rachana
